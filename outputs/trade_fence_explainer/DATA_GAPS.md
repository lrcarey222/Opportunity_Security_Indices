# Data gaps

What the package lacks, why it matters, the best source, and whether the charts stand without it.

| # | Gap | Why it matters | Best authoritative source | Charts still produced? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Census monthly data after August 2026.** The Trade War Tracker's files stop at 2026-08 (commit a9063f35, 6 Oct 2026). | Observed duties miss Section 338 basket changes (15 Sep, 30 Oct), the India/Indonesia/Laos solar deposits (5 Nov) and Proclamation 11052 (4 Dec). These enter only as modelled statutory changes. | Census International Trade API (needs a key; the project holds none) or USITC DataWeb | Yes: Dec 4 layer is modelled and labelled as such |
| 2 | **Partner detail outside the tracker's 30 partners** (Cambodia, Laos, Philippines, Ethiopia, Kenya, Nigeria...). | 80% of Jan-Aug 2026 c-Si module imports come from untracked countries. | Census API by partner at HS10 (key needed) | Yes: chart 8A splits the Census remainder by UN Comtrade HS6 shares (incl. thin film); the HS10 c-Si split for those countries is estimated |
| 3 | **Minimum import prices as tariff equivalents** (Proclamation 11052: $21/kg polysilicon, $100/kg wafers, $0.22/W cells, $0.38/W modules). | The floor may bind on cheap imports (Census-derived module unit values were about $0.18/W in Q1 2026 per NREL), which would make the MIP far more restrictive than the 15% tariff. | Entry-level $/W prices; CBP has issued no MIP entry guidance as of 9 Nov 2026 | Yes: flagged, not converted (charts 2/4/5 notes; snapshot rows) |
| 4 | **Section 338 Canada basket coverage.** Annexes of Procs 11046-11048, 11064-11065 and the 23 Oct 2026 proclamation are images in the Federal Register; we rely on the tracker's extraction. | Drives 6-12 points of the trusted-partner TRI in wind, solar and grid assembly on 4 Dec (chart 7). | HTSUS chapter 99, U.S. note 51 and headings 9903.03.12-14 (USITC HTS revision after 30 Oct 2026) | Yes, with a caveat in chart 7 |
| 5 | **Large power transformers from Korea AD order** (A-580-867) is not in `adcvd_orders.csv`. | Korea supplies ~18% of US liquid-dielectric transformer imports; deposits are company-specific. Grid assembly trusted-partner rate is understated. | Commerce ACCESS / latest administrative review final results | Yes; not modelled |
| 6 | **Section 232 trucks, semiconductors and drones** are not in the tracker engine. | Understates EV assembly (electric trucks) and any basket goods on those lists; their goods also wrongly receive stacked 301/338 duties. | Proclamations 10984, 11002 and the UAS proclamation annexes | Yes; listed in the snapshot as not modelled |
| 7 | **Engine simplifications**: auto-parts 232 applied to every good in a listed provision (all Li-ion batteries, switchgear 8537.10); Canada/Mexico parts treated at 15%. | Engine-only statutory levels run above observed; chart 7 is therefore anchored to observed rates. | Tracker METHODOLOGY.md open issues | Yes |
| 8 | **AD/CVD company-specific rates and circumvention rulings.** All-others rates only. | Large exporters often pay less; circumvention orders extend coverage to third countries. | Commerce cash-deposit instructions (CBP ADCVD messages) | Yes |
| 9 | **Wafers in 2024 trade data**: Proclamation 11052 covers 3818.00.0020/0040/0045/0050/0091, which in 2024 were all inside 3818.00.0090 with uncovered wafers. | The 15% duty on the whole 2024 line overstates wafers (about half of the line's 2026 value is uncovered). | n/a (classification change) | Yes; upper bound noted |
| 10 | **US module production.** No official series after 2022 (EIA-63B suspended after Sep 2024 and proposed for discontinuation; it reports shipments incl. imports). | Chart 8A shows capacity, not output. | EIA-63B (if reinstated); otherwise Wood Mackenzie/SEIA (secondary) | Yes: operating capacity (SEIA/WoodMac) shown, production quoted from NREL decks with vintage caveat |
| 11 | **US lithium-ion cell production and utilisation.** No credible output series; capacity only (Rhodium/MIT CIM: 202 GWh Q1 2025, 392 GWh Q3 2026). | Cannot show reshoring of battery output. | DOE/Argonne Li-ion supply chain database (capacity); no official output series | Panel B shows trade and ownership only |
| 12 | **Ownership of battery capacity (GWh) abroad.** CM-FDI records projects and disclosed capital since about 2017, not installed capacity; several large projects (CATL, CALB in Thailand) have no disclosed capital. | Chinese-parent shares are of projects/capital, not of capacity or exports to the US. | Benchmark Mineral Intelligence or similar plant-level capacity databases (commercial) | Yes, with caveat |
| 13 | **Fence-tools workbook pipeline** (Python, NIPO October 2026 extract) is vendored but not ported into the R pipeline; chart 4 reads frozen copies of its derived tables (sha256 in the README of the build). | Re-running chart 4 on a new NIPO extract needs the Python scripts in `scripts/trade_fence_explainer/fence_tools/`. | GTA NIPO | Yes |
| 14 | **Taiwan** has no UN General Assembly vote, so it has no alignment position. | Dropped from chart 3 (still in every TRI as nonaligned). | n/a | Yes |
| 15 | **Independent re-check of the Proclamation 11052 reading** was cut short by a session usage limit; the primary research read the Federal Register text (91 FR 51975) and annex images directly. Open points it flagged: the heading-number clash with the quartz safeguard (9903.45.30-31), thin-film coverage, whether onshoring relief waives the MIP, and whether cells/modules are exempt from the forced-labour 301 duty (primary text says no; law-firm alerts say yes). | The 301 point changes the Dec 4 module rate by 10-12.5 points. | HTSUS revision implementing note 42; USTR clarification | Yes; engine follows the primary text (301 applies to cells and modules) |
