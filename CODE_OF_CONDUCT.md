# Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone who participates in this project.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate in words and actions.
- Welcoming different perspectives and experiences.
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.

## Reporting

To report a concern, please contact the project maintainers via the contact information in the repository README or open a private report through GitHub security advisories if available. We will review reports promptly and keep information confidential to the extent possible.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may take any action they deem appropriate, including removing content, temporarily or permanently restricting participation, or escalating to relevant authorities when necessary.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, and community discussions.
