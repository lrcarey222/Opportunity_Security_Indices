## Energy security risks and why

Of France's {{summary.n_placed}} placed sectors, those comparable with other economies, {{summary.n_insecure}} sit in the two low-energy-security corners. Of these, {{summary.quadrants.upgrade}} are in the Upgrade corner, which pairs high opportunity with low energy security, and {{summary.quadrants.secure}} in the Secure corner, where both are low. The paper's framework, after RMI (2025), associates the Upgrade corner with building domestic capability and the Secure corner with securing supply through trusted partners.

Energy Security measures how well a country can rely on what it needs. Its lowest Energy Security percentiles are in {{cells.oil-downstream.label_prose}}, {{cells.coal-downstream.label_prose}}, {{cells.nuclear-upstream.label_prose}} and {{cells.oil-upstream.label_prose}}. In the downstream sectors it scores higher than only {{cells.oil-downstream.es.pct}} and {{cells.coal-downstream.es.pct}} of the other {{cells.oil-downstream.es.n_others}} economies scored, and both sit in the {{cells.oil-downstream.quadrant}} corner. Low {{cells.oil-downstream.drivers.es.consumption.category|lower}} scores pull them down: on expected growth in use per person, it scores higher than only {{cells.oil-downstream.drivers.es.consumption.vars.elec-growth-index.pct}} and {{cells.coal-downstream.drivers.es.consumption.vars.elec-growth-index.pct}} of the other economies.

Its high-priority sectors, the high-opportunity, low-energy-security ones that the Strategic Index ranks highest, are led by {{cells.nuclear-upstream.label_prose}}. There its Energy Security score of {{cells.nuclear-upstream.es}} is close to the sector's median of {{cells.nuclear-upstream.es.med}}, yet higher than only {{cells.nuclear-upstream.es.pct}} of the other {{cells.nuclear-upstream.es.n_others}} economies scored. On its trade deficit in these goods, {{cells.nuclear-upstream.drivers.es.trade.vars.deficit-gdp.raw}} in {{cells.nuclear-upstream.drivers.es.trade.vars.deficit-gdp.raw.year}}, it scores higher than only {{cells.nuclear-upstream.drivers.es.trade.vars.deficit-gdp.pct}} of the other economies. For context, its largest suppliers in {{meta.trade_year}} were {{cells.nuclear-upstream.trade.sources.0.name}} ({{cells.nuclear-upstream.trade.sources.0.share}} of its imports) and {{cells.nuclear-upstream.trade.sources.1.name}} ({{cells.nuclear-upstream.trade.sources.1.share}}).

Its other high-priority sectors are {{cells.gas-upstream.label_prose}} and {{cells.oil-upstream.label_prose}}. Both score low mainly because of {{cells.oil-upstream.drivers.es.energy-imports.category|lower}} and {{cells.oil-upstream.drivers.es.reserves.category|lower}}. Production minus consumption was {{cells.oil-upstream.drivers.es.energy-imports.vars.production-surplus-deficit.raw}} for oil and {{cells.gas-upstream.drivers.es.energy-imports.vars.production-surplus-deficit.raw}} for gas in {{cells.gas-upstream.drivers.es.energy-imports.vars.production-surplus-deficit.raw.year}}. ## Economic opportunities and why

France's openings are broad: {{summary.n_high_eo}} of its placed sectors sit in the two high-opportunity corners, {{summary.quadrants.grow}} of them in the Grow corner, which pairs high opportunity with high energy security. The paper's framework, after RMI (2025), associates the Grow corner with export promotion.

Economic Opportunity measures how well placed a country is to create value. Its highest Economic Opportunity percentiles are in {{cells.nuclear-midstream.label_prose}}, {{cells.nuclear-downstream.label_prose}}, {{cells.gas-midstream.label_prose}} and {{cells.batteries-downstream.label_prose}}. It scores higher than {{cells.nuclear-midstream.eo.pct}}, {{cells.nuclear-downstream.eo.pct}}, {{cells.gas-midstream.eo.pct}} and {{cells.batteries-downstream.eo.pct}} of the other economies scored in these sectors, respectively.

In {{cells.nuclear-midstream.label_prose}}, the score is high mainly because of {{cells.nuclear-midstream.drivers.eo.trade.category|lower}} and {{cells.nuclear-midstream.drivers.eo.cost-competitiveness.category|lower}}. In {{cells.nuclear-downstream.label_prose}}, {{cells.nuclear-downstream.drivers.eo.investment.category|lower}} and {{cells.nuclear-downstream.drivers.eo.production.category|lower}} push the score up: it generated {{cells.nuclear-downstream.drivers.eo.production.vars.size.raw}} in {{cells.nuclear-downstream.drivers.eo.production.vars.size.raw.year}}, a measure on which it scores higher than {{cells.nuclear-downstream.drivers.eo.production.vars.size.pct}} of the other economies.

In {{cells.nuclear-upstream.label_prose}}, its Economic Opportunity is higher than {{cells.nuclear-upstream.eo.pct}} of the other economies scored, mainly because of {{cells.nuclear-upstream.drivers.eo.trade.category|lower}} and {{cells.nuclear-upstream.drivers.eo.cost-competitiveness.category|lower}}.
