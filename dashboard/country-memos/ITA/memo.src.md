## Energy security risks and why

Of Italy's {{summary.n_placed}} placed sectors, those comparable with other economies, {{summary.n_insecure}} sit in the two low-energy-security corners. Of these, {{summary.quadrants.upgrade}} are in the Upgrade corner, which pairs high opportunity with low energy security, and {{summary.quadrants.secure}} in the Secure corner, where both are low. The paper's framework, after RMI (2025), associates the Upgrade corner with building domestic capability and the Secure corner with securing supply through trusted partners.

Energy Security measures how well a country can rely on what it needs. Its lowest Energy Security percentiles are in {{cells.coal-downstream.label_prose}}, {{cells.coal-upstream.label_prose}}, {{cells.nuclear-downstream.label_prose}} and {{cells.gas-upstream.label_prose}}. It scores higher than only {{cells.coal-downstream.es.pct}}, {{cells.coal-upstream.es.pct}}, {{cells.nuclear-downstream.es.pct}} and {{cells.gas-upstream.es.pct}} of the other economies scored, respectively. In {{cells.coal-downstream.label_prose}}, in the {{cells.coal-downstream.quadrant}} corner, the score is low mainly because of {{cells.coal-downstream.drivers.es.energy-access.category|lower}}. Its consumption in this sector was {{cells.coal-downstream.drivers.es.energy-access.vars.energy-consumption-per-capita.raw}} in {{cells.coal-downstream.drivers.es.energy-access.vars.energy-consumption-per-capita.raw.year}}, and its change was {{cells.coal-downstream.drivers.es.energy-access.vars.energy-consumption-per-capita-growth.raw}}, a measure on which it scores higher than only {{cells.coal-downstream.drivers.es.energy-access.vars.energy-consumption-per-capita-growth.pct}} of the other economies.

In {{cells.gas-upstream.label_prose}}, in the {{cells.gas-upstream.quadrant}} corner, the score is low mainly because of {{cells.gas-upstream.drivers.es.energy-imports.category|lower}} and {{cells.gas-upstream.drivers.es.trade.category|lower}}. ## Economic opportunities and why

The paper's framework, after RMI (2025), associates the Grow corner with export promotion.

Its highest Economic Opportunity percentiles are in {{cells.gas-midstream.label_prose}}, {{cells.oil-midstream.label_prose}}, {{cells.batteries-downstream.label_prose}} and {{cells.coal-midstream.label_prose}}.
