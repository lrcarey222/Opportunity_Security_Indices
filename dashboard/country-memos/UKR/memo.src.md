## Energy security risks and why

The Secure corner, which pairs low energy security with low opportunity, holds all {{summary.quadrants.secure}} of them. Against other economies in the same sector, its Energy Security is lowest in {{cells.solar-downstream.label_prose}}, {{cells.nuclear-midstream.label_prose}} and {{cells.wind-downstream.label_prose}}. ## Economic opportunities and why

Openings are scarce: only {{summary.n_high_eo}} of {{meta.country}}'s placed sectors, {{cells.green-hydrogen-downstream.label_prose}}, sits in the two high-opportunity corners. Economic Opportunity measures how well placed a country is to create value in a sector, from exports and market share to investment, costs, demand and related capabilities.

Against other economies in the same sector, its Economic Opportunity is highest in {{cells.green-hydrogen-downstream.label_prose}} ({{cells.green-hydrogen-downstream.quadrant}} corner), {{cells.nuclear-downstream.label_prose}} ({{cells.nuclear-downstream.quadrant}}) and {{cells.oil-downstream.label_prose}} ({{cells.oil-downstream.quadrant}}). Its scores there are higher than {{cells.green-hydrogen-downstream.eo.pct}}, {{cells.nuclear-downstream.eo.pct}} and {{cells.oil-downstream.eo.pct}} of the other economies scored.

In {{cells.green-hydrogen-downstream.label_prose}}, consumption pushes the score up: on use per person today it scores higher than {{cells.green-hydrogen-downstream.drivers.eo.consumption.vars.installed-cap-index.pct}} of the other economies, and on expected growth in use per person higher than {{cells.green-hydrogen-downstream.drivers.eo.consumption.vars.elec-growth-index.pct}}.

In {{cells.nuclear-downstream.label_prose}}, investment pushes the score up: on annual investment it scores higher than {{cells.nuclear-downstream.drivers.eo.investment.vars.annual-investment-index.pct}} of the other economies. Cost competitiveness pulls it down: on input costs for labour and capital it scores higher than only {{cells.nuclear-downstream.drivers.eo.cost-competitiveness.vars.input-cost-index.pct}}.

In {{cells.oil-downstream.label_prose}}, production pushes the score up: power generation from oil was {{cells.oil-downstream.drivers.eo.production.vars.size.raw}} in {{cells.oil-downstream.drivers.eo.production.vars.size.raw.year}}, and on growth in production it scores higher than {{cells.oil-downstream.drivers.eo.production.vars.growth-abs.pct}} of the other economies.
