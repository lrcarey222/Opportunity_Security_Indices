## Energy security risks and why

Of the United Kingdom's {{summary.n_placed}} placed sectors, those comparable with other economies, {{summary.n_insecure}} sit in the two low-energy-security corners. All {{summary.quadrants.secure}} are in the Secure corner, which pairs low energy security with low opportunity. The paper's framework, after RMI (2025), associates the Secure corner with securing supply through trusted partners.

Energy Security measures how well a country can rely on what it needs. Its lowest Energy Security percentiles are in {{cells.coal-downstream.label_prose}}, {{cells.oil-downstream.label_prose}}, {{cells.gas-downstream.label_prose}} and {{cells.nuclear-upstream.label_prose}}. There its Energy Security score of {{cells.nuclear-upstream.es}} sits just below the sector's median of {{cells.nuclear-upstream.es.med}}, in the {{cells.nuclear-upstream.quadrant}} corner. The gap to the median mainly reflects its {{cells.nuclear-upstream.drivers.es.trade.category|lower}} category.
