# Korea–Australia Strategic Energy & Industrial Partnership

*Where deeper industrial integration creates strategic value, through which instruments, and where to stay out*

*Generated from Allied Industrial Base Explorer data and a curated external evidence layer (accessed 2026-11-06); evidence, sources and caveats in the appendix. Data as of 30 October 2026.*

## Executive Decision Summary

- **Build together.** Permanent Magnets & Drive Systems: Electric Vehicles components (Korea->Australia). Wind & Hydropower Equipment: Wind towers (Korea->Australia). Battery Materials & Cells: Batteries assembly (Korea->Australia). Grid Equipment & Conductors: Power cables (Korea->Australia). Electric Vehicles: Electric Vehicles assembly (Korea->Australia).
- **Trade and standards.** Common anti-circumvention rules for solar refining (copper wire & rod, polysilicon) and batteries refining (battery materials (foil, electrolyte), cathode materials & precursors, cobalt intermediates & chemicals) and electric vehicles refining (rare-earth metals, rare-earth oxides & compounds). Bind market access for batteries assembly (li-ion cells & packs, other accumulators), batteries components (accumulator parts & separators), electric vehicles components (accumulator parts & separators, electric motors, ferrite & other magnets), wind towers, wind assembly (electricity meters, overhead conductors, power cables), power cables. Keep nuclear special materials; battery recycling feed; semiconductor manufacturing equipment open to global sourcing.
- **Institutions.** Upgrade EFA-KEXIM co-financing channel for critical minerals: sector strategy, reciprocal commitments, a KPI dashboard and an annual review. No new MOU.
- **Stay out.** Do not create a new bilateral energy MOU or a free-standing MEA institution. Do not put new public money behind Batteries extraction (cobalt ores, lithium minerals, natural graphite) investment that is already committed (evidence KRAU-14. Do not put new public money behind Batteries refining (battery materials (foil, electrolyte), cathode materials & precursors, cobalt intermediates & chemicals) investment that is already committed (evidence KRAU-11.

**Why this partnership matters.**

- Of 142 technology x stage cells and electro-industrial nodes assessed for the pair, 25 show an active complementarity (a supplier meeting the other's need, a shared chokepoint, a capacity-constrained trusted supplier or a chain specialisation), 0 direct competition and 90 no strategic case. The relationship is not one of general similarity: most of the electro-industrial base needs no bilateral intervention. [1]
- AIB strategic roles: Korea Specialized Technology & Manufacturing Anchor (primary), Scale Manufacturing & Integration Platform (primary), Coordination / Bridge Hub (secondary), Capital & Investment Anchor (secondary); Australia Resource & Processing Anchor (primary). [1]

**The central bilateral bargain.**

- *Inference. *Australia supplies rare-earth metals, lithium minerals where Korea is short; Korea supplies wind towers, power cables, cathode materials & precursors, accumulator parts & separators where Australia is short; they share no node-level chokepoint where neither supplies.
- *Inference. *From the AIB roles, Korea brings specialised technology and manufacturing, scale manufacturing, coordination across allied chains; Australia brings resources and processing. Public finance runs through KEXIM, K-SURE, KOMIR, KDB on the Korea side and EFA, NRFC, CEFC, ARENA on the Australia side. The trade is market access and demand certainty for trusted capacity.

## 10. What Not to Do

- *Recommendation. ***N01** Do not create a new bilateral energy MOU or a free-standing MEA institution: KRAU-M3 already exists (finance and projects but no published list); dock the compacts in it.
- *Recommendation. ***N02** Do not put new public money behind Batteries extraction (cobalt ores, lithium minerals, natural graphite) investment that is already committed (evidence KRAU-14; KRAU-19; KRAU-20); support is additional only beyond it.
- *Recommendation. ***N03** Do not put new public money behind Batteries refining (battery materials (foil, electrolyte), cathode materials & precursors, cobalt intermediates & chemicals) investment that is already committed (evidence KRAU-11; KRAU-12; KRAU-13; KRAU-21); support is additional only beyond it.
- *Recommendation. ***N04** Do not put new public money behind Electric Vehicles extraction (cobalt ores, lithium minerals, natural graphite) investment that is already committed (evidence USAU-09; KRAU-14; KRAU-19; KRAU-20); support is additional only beyond it.
- *Recommendation. ***N05** Do not defend AC generators as a bilateral priority: trusted supply is adequate (AIB LOW INTERVENTION), yet Australia's observed protection or attention is high.
- *Recommendation. ***N06** Do not defend Automatic controllers as a bilateral priority: trusted supply is adequate (AIB LOW INTERVENTION), yet Korea's observed protection or attention is high.
