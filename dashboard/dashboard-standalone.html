Normalisation

Scores are normalised using a median-centred S-curve: idx = r^γ / (r^γ + (1−r)^γ) where r is the percentile rank and γ = 0.5. This compresses the distribution near the median while preserving separation at the tails, rewarding true leaders and penalising laggards more than a linear ranking.
